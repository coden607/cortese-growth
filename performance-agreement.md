# PERFORMANCE RECOVERY AGREEMENT (one-pager — fill brackets, send)

**This Agreement** is between **Cortese Digital** ("Consultant") and **[CLIENT LEGAL NAME]** ("Client"), effective [DATE].

## 1. Scope
Consultant builds and operates revenue-recovery workflows for Client's business: failed-payment recovery (dunning), abandoned-cart recovery, dead-lead reactivation, churned-customer win-back, and no-show follow-up sequences (the "Workflows"). Consultant selects which levers to pull based on Client's leak audit.

## 2. Compensation — Performance Only
- Consultant earns **[25]% of Recovered Revenue**, defined as revenue actually collected by Client that is attributed to the Workflows via the tracking in Section 3, **above the Baseline** in Section 4.
- **No recovery, no fee.** If the Workflows recover nothing in a billing period, Client owes $0 for that period.
- Optional structure for Clients who demand a floor: **[${1,500}/month minimum]**, credited against earned percentage fees.

## 3. Attribution & Tracking
- Recovered Revenue is tracked via [dedicated recovery links / promo codes / payment-recovery platform reports / tagged sequences agreed in writing at kickoff].
- Consultant provides a monthly attribution report. Client may audit underlying records on 5 business days' notice.

## 4. Baseline
- Baseline = the average monthly revenue from the recovered streams over the 90 days preceding kickoff ("Pre-Baseline Period"), agreed in the Kickoff Sheet (Exhibit A).
- One-time recovered payments (e.g., a single large failed invoice) count at [50]% toward fees if outside the recurring stream.

## 5. Term & Exit
- **Pilot:** 90 days from kickoff. Either party may exit at day 90 with 7 days' written notice.
- After pilot: month-to-month, same terms, either party exits with 14 days' notice.
- On exit: Workflows and assets built for Client remain Client's; Consultant retains reusable methods/templates.

## 6. The Honest Clauses (protect both sides)
- **No guarantee:** Consultant does not promise a specific recovery amount. The fee structure exists because results are the only billable output.
- **Client duties:** provide access (payment processor, email/SMS platform, cart/store, past customer lists) within 7 days of kickoff, and honor recovered purchases per normal policies.
- **Payment:** fees invoiced monthly, due net 15. Late fees: 1.5%/month.
- **Liability cap:** Consultant's total liability ≤ fees paid in the prior 3 months. No indirect/consequential damages either direction.
- **Confidentiality:** mutual, standard, survives 12 months post-term.
- **Relationship:** independent contractor. No exclusivity unless added in Exhibit B.
