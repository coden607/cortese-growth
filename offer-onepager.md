# OFFER ONE-PAGER — "The Recovery Deal"

**The pitch in one breath:**
> "You are already losing money every month — failed payments, abandoned carts, dead leads, customers who churned and never heard from you again. I build the workflows that recover it. I don't charge a retainer. I take a percentage of what I actually put back in your bank account. No recovery, no fee."

## Why performance pricing closes
- **Zero-risk frame:** prospect pays nothing upfront; fee comes out of new-found money, never their budget.
- **Math they feel:** if their leak is $8k/month and you recover half, your 25% = $1k — they keep $3k they were otherwise losing forever.
- **Objection pre-emption:** "What if it doesn't work?" → "Then you paid nothing and you keep the workflows I built."

## Numbers to anchor (always label as estimates in conversation)
- Typical leak: 3–9% of revenue sitting in failed payments + abandoned carts + dead leads (ASSUMPTION ranges — never quote as fact).
- Dunning alone recovers 30–70% of failed payments when automated properly (ASSUMPTION).
- Offer: **25% of recovered revenue, 90-day pilot, month-to-month after.** Bigger clients: 20% + $1.5k/mo floor.

## Who says YES fastest (priority order)
1. **Subscription e-commerce (Shopify apps: Recharge, Skio, Appstle)** — failed recurring payments = instant recoverable cash. Search: BuiltWith/StoreLeads for stores w/ subscription tech, or "subscribe & save" stores running Meta ads.
2. **Agencies & B2B services sitting on dead pipelines** — old proposals + cold lead lists = reactivation wins.
3. **Med-spas / dental / elective medical** — no-shows + consult no-book follow-ups; high ticket sizes.
4. **Gyms / memberships** — lapsed-member win-back.
5. **SaaS <$2M ARR** — churned-trial and failed-payment recovery.
