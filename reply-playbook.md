# Reply Playbook — Cortese Digital campaign inbox (coden607@gmail.com)

Triage every reply within 2 business hours. Three lanes:

## 🟢 INTERESTED ("tell me more" / "how does it work" / "send info")

Reply same hour. Short + concrete:

> Thanks [name]! Easiest next step: I'll text you the demo link — 30 seconds and you'll see exactly how the busy-line text-back works. When's good to walk through it? I can also stop by the counter — I demo in 20 minutes, no prep on your end.
>
> — Stephen

Then: send demo link, log in lead tracker, propose 2 concrete times (counter demo or call).

## 🟡 CURIOUS SKEPTIC ("how much" / "we already have online ordering" / "sounds too good")

Answer the question in ONE line, then re-offer the demo:

- **How much?** → "Zero upfront, zero monthly. I bill 15% of orders the system actually recovers — no recovered orders, no charge."
- **We have online ordering** → "This is for the people who never see your website — the ones who call and hit the busy signal. That's the leak it plugs."
- **Too good** → "The risk story is inverted on purpose: if it saves nothing, you pay nothing. There's no way to lose money on it."

Then: "The 30-second demo shows it better than I can explain it: [link]. Worth a look?"

## 🔴 STOP / OPT-OUT

Reply once to confirm, no pitch content:

> Understood — you're off the list, no further contact. (And if your Friday rush ever eats a caller, you know where to find me.)

Then: add address to suppression list (never email again), mark lead `suppressed` in tracker.

## HARD RULES

1. **Never argue** — one answer, then pivot to demo or exit gracefully.
2. **Demo link in every non-STOP reply** — it's the closer.
3. **Log every reply** in the tracker: date, lane, next action.
4. **STOP = permanent suppression** across all channels.
